Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1 November 2026
Developer: Nahla Labs

Nahla Labs develops privacy-first mobile applications and games, including Sulphur Run: Pâte Sablée Descent. This policy outlines how our applications interact with your device and personal data.

1. Information Collection and Telemetry

Nahla Labs does not collect, monitor, transmit, store, or share any personal information, personally identifiable data (PII), or device telemetry. Our games are built to operate completely offline.

2. Local Device Storage & Data Retention

All high scores, gameplay preferences, audio settings, and session states are recorded solely on your local device via client-side storage. This data is never synchronized with external servers or cloud repositories.

You retain full control over this data. You can erase all locally stored game information at any time by clearing the application storage via Android system settings or by uninstalling the application.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Our applications contain no third-party software development kits (SDKs), embedded tracking mechanisms, analytics suites, or third-party advertising frameworks.
