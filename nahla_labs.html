Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1st November 2026
Developer: Nahla Labs

Nahla Labs develops privacy-first mobile applications, analytical tools, research environments, and games. This policy applies across our software suite, including Sulphur Run: Pâte Sablée Descent, Thesis Memory Web, Laniakea, Nahla Tracker, and standalone utilities. It outlines how our software interacts with your device and data.

1. Information Collection and Telemetry

Nahla Labs does not harvest, monitor, compile, sell, or profile your personal information, personally identifiable data (PII), or device telemetry. We embed no analytical surveillance, user tracking scripts, crash log harvesters, or behaviour monitoring tools across our software.

2. Data Storage, Research Data & Intellectual Property

Depending on the specific tool or application used, data is stored either locally on your device or synchronised securely via cloud infrastructure:

  • Offline Applications & Games: For standalone tools, offline games (including Sulphur Run: Pâte Sablée Descent), and client-side utilities, all high scores, gameplay states, and audio settings are recorded strictly on your local device.
  • Connected Research Environments: For web applications utilising online persistence, user data and qualitative graph nodes are hosted via Google Firebase / Firestore solely to provide multi-device persistence and state management. Nahla Labs does not read, inspect, analyse, share, or monetise your research notes, nodes, imported/exported files, or personal intellectual property.

You retain complete and unencumbered ownership of your intellectual property and data at all times. Local data can be erased at any time by clearing your browser cache or application data, and users can export their data directly to local disk (via JSON, Markdown, or GEXF) at will[cite: 1].

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Usage

Our applications only access device hardware features that are fundamentally required for functional output (such as audio rendering channels for soundboards and synthesisers, or canvas display rendering). We do not request, access, or log sensitive device permissions, including microphone inputs, cameras, precise GPS location, contacts, or external account identities.

4. Third-Party Services, Infrastructure & External Links

Our applications contain no third-party marketing software development kits (SDKs), advertising networks, or tracking suites. Where cloud infrastructure (such as Google Firebase) is utilised to host database records for web utilities, data is handled subject to standard Google Cloud infrastructure security standards.

Certain web pages contain outbound links to external services (such as YouTube or Inara). Clicking these links takes you to external platforms governed by their own independent privacy notices and terms.
